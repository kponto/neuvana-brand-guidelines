## What Neuvana is

- A consumer wellness company. Positioning is consumer wellness: sleep, stress, focus. No medical or Rx claims.
- Product: the Neuvana Xen, earbuds that deliver non-invasive vagus nerve stimulation through the ear while the user listens to music. The app is required to pair (iOS/Android). HSA/FSA eligible. No prescription.
- How stakeholders describe it: “Health and wellness company” / “Electrical stimulation” (Tracy); “Health tech” (Ami); “Wellness - biohacker” (Cassie); “non-pharma approach” (Dave).
- Differentiators the team names: “Music”, “You don't need to change your habits”, “It's active, not just monitoring” (Kermit); “DISCREET” (Kermit); “Discrete. Not goofy.” and “Elegant.” (Tracy); “Doesn't look weird” (Ami).

## What Neuvana is not

- Not pharma: “We're not pharma!” (Brand Readout); Ami lists “Not: Pharma”.
- Not a “medical device” in consumer communication (Cassie).
- Not a technology company (Tracy). This conflicts with Ami’s “Health tech”; see Category claim.
- Not a monitor: “Other devices in consumer health tech only monitor - but they don't do anything about it” (Kermit).
- “we're not a quick fix” and “We're not fast-fashion” (Cassie); “it's not a cure-all” (Dave); “not just a zap and done” (workshop).

## Competitive frame

Consumer competitors named in the June 2026 positioning set: Pulsetto, Truvaga, VeRelief Prime / Hoolest, Apollo Neuro, Sensate, Muse, DUSQ and AlphaStim. Ami’s competitor list: “mattress companies, digital wearables, supplements.”

| Competitor | Difference, in source language |
|---|---|
| Pulsetto | “makes all the outrageous claims”, “They're always on sale”, “They hid all the bad reviews” (Cassie). Ami: “They own the market.” Intended contrast: no overclaiming, and rare discounts. |
| Sensate | A tonal reference, not a contrast: “trusted, legit, not aggressive - credible” (Ami). |
| Truvaga / electroCore | “has consumer and medical devices” (Dave). |
| Apollo Neuro | Wrist-worn. |
| Wearables (Oura, Whoop) | They only monitor; Xen is active (Kermit). |
| DUSQ | Behind-the-ear VNS plus vestibular stimulation for sleep. |

Device-experience map: Neuvana is plotted discreet and easy. Pulsetto, Sensate, Truvaga / Hoolest and Muse are plotted easy but conspicuous. Apollo Neuro is plotted discreet and easy.

Known weakness: “Competitors don't have an app - that adds to complexity - they're more straightforward.” (Kermit)
