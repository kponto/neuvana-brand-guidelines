# Art direction — graphics

**Status:** draft.

## Editorial filter (pass/fail)

“order in the container, life (and experimentation!) in the content”. If both container and content are loose, the result reads as wellness mush. If both are strict, it reads as pharma.

## AI generation

- **Use for:** environments, objects, skies, textures, extending real photographs, and concepting.
- **Not for:** people presented as real customers or users, or device renders shown in trust-bearing contexts.
- Disclose AI imagery and apply platform labels.

## Illustration

- A supporting role only: mechanism diagrams, app spot illustrations, experiments.
- If a house style is needed, use line work derived from the logo.
- Avoid rendered vector realism, which “reads as clip art”.

## Data and signal graphics

- Real data only: HRV charts, real stimulation waveforms, real session structures, monospace labels, timestamps. No decorative or fake data.
- Template: the spec-sheet layout (spec-sheet t-shirt).
- Geometric abstraction derived from session structure.
- Signal motif: “noise resolving to coherence” was recommended as the visual platform. Kevin’s direction: “something very stylized. Maybe not even immediately recognizable as a signal per se.” [INCOMPLETE — not resolved]

## Abstract fields

Grainy gradient fields, macro natural materials, sky, water, light and shadow.

## Music

- Texture, never the subject. Test: “if a composition would still work as a headphone ad, it's over the line.”
- Never: equalizer bars, floating notes, neon soundwaves, vinyl or cassette nostalgia, lyrics.

## Promotions

“Discounts should be rare and typographic, never the headline of a lifestyle image.” Reason: Pulsetto is “always on sale”, and a brand built on trust cannot look like a clearance feed.

## Experimental lane

About 15% of the mix: humor, surrealism, illustration, collage, music-driven concepts. Each experiment is tracked, and the ones that work graduate into the default mix. The lane is reviewed quarterly.
