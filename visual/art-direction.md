# Art direction — photography

**Status:** draft. Exclusions below are recommended defaults. [INCOMPLETE — checklist results from the 2026-09-30 meeting with Ami are not in the source materials.]

## Principle

“The Frame carries the brand, so the imagery inside it doesn't have to.” The system is a short list of hard exclusions, a default content mix and an experimental lane. “Everything not excluded is permitted.”

## Default content mix (starting proportions)

- About 50% documentary photography of ordinary life
- About 20% signal and data graphics
- About 15% abstract, gradient and texture fields
- About 15% experimental work
- Studio product photography sits outside the mix. It is a separate spec for commerce surfaces. [INCOMPLETE — studio spec not written]

## Subjects

- Real people in present-tense situations: “commuting, desk work, pre-event nerves, evening wind-down, the 3am wake.”
- Ordinary interiors are the primary register. Close-range physical sensation (shoulders, hands, jaw, the side of a neck with the cable running out of frame) is the detail register.
- Night and threshold scenes are a defined subset for sleep and evening content.
- Posture and physical structure are shown “as the body rather than as anatomy.”
- Frames within the frame (windows, doorways, thresholds) appear in “roughly one in four images.”

## Casting

- Core cast 45–65, secondary cast 30s.
- Non-professional talent, visible age, real bodies, unstyled hair, minimal retouching.
- Expression: “relief, not bliss.” Relieved and downshifted. Not blissful, not posed, not stressed.

## Device rule

When a person is the subject, either the device is visible or the caption names the session. Earbuds and cable should be visible in most lifestyle frames.

## Light

A single visible light source with readable direction and retained shadow. Window light and lamplight do most of the work. No on-camera flash.

## Grade and materials

- Color Negative 400 base, with warmth pulled into the highlights and softer contrast in skin. Delivered as one LUT.
- The hazy Vintage Analog look is allowed only as an accent for sleep and evening content.
- Grain is matched to the gradient panels.
- Accent colors appear only as real objects in the frame, never as a tint.
- Materials: wood, linen, ceramic, worn cotton, unpainted plaster.

## Composition

Off-center subjects, tight body crops, incidental framing, mid-gesture moments, negative space inside the image. For the container rules, see visual-system.json.

## Captions

Documentation register: “factual, timestamped, specific.” Example: “Second session. 11:40pm.”

## Never

Lotus meditation; brain or nerve diagrams; electricity or glow graphics; before/after; product floating on white seamless; stock stress poses; lab coats; beach sunsets; gym coding; spa props; brand-color tints or duotone; headphone-ad compositions; “Beatific closed-eye serenity”.
