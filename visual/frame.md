# The Frame

The primary visual identifier. A rigid container that affords wide internal content variation while remaining branded. Metaphor: a regulated system (the nervous system + Neuvana) affords internal sway (a rich, full life). Order in the container, life in the content.

The formats below are structural. They are not tied to a size. A business card and an 8-foot banner can use the same format. Instagram 1080×1350 is one application of these formats, not the definition.

## Color

The color of the Frame is chosen to contrast the content. Lighter content, darker Frame. Darker content, lighter Frame.

## Formats

### Masthead

A distinct header region sits at the top of the Frame and holds the type and logo. The content well fills the rest. The header is part of the container, not an overlay. There is no locked ratio for the header height.

Logo is typically centered, with centered text underneath, unless the application requires otherwise. The Anywhere banners are the exception examples.

The Cafe, Airplane, and Office banners are Masthead instances, not a separate format.

### Polaroid

A distinct footer region sits at the bottom of the Frame and holds the type and logo. The content well fills the area above it. The footer is part of the container. There is no locked ratio for the footer height.

Text is left aligned. The logo is right aligned.

### Poster

No separate bar. The content well is the full interior of the Frame. Type and logo overlay the content.

Placements in use: top centered, bottom centered, middle left, middle right.

## Instagram master (size-specific)

These numbers apply only to the 1080×1350 social master. They are not part of the format definitions.

- Canvas: 1080×1350 (4:5)
- Outer border: 60px
- Polaroid and Masthead well: 960×990
- Poster well: 960×1230

## Video

Video can be full bleed for now and is not subject to the Frame at this time.
